Dish Profile

Screen S4

Description

Information screen for a single dish. Accessible to both Planners and Members. Opened by tapping a day card in S2 (member mode) or a dish row in S5 (dishes manager).

Tracked Metrics

  • Last Cooked — days elapsed since last serving. Powers the rotation score on S3.
  • Times Cooked — cumulative cook count, auto-incremented on day rollover.

v1 Scope Note

Ingredients and notes are plain read-only text. Grocery checklists, ingredient quantities, and recipe steps are out of scope for v1 and are v2 candidates.

Entry Points

  • S2 Week View → tap a dish card (member)
  • S5 Dishes Manager → tap a dish row
